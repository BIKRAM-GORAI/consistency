Cancellation & Refund Policy

Thank you for subscribing to Consistency Daily Premium! We aim to provide an Elite habit building routine. Please review our compliant payment cancellation guidelines below:

  • Subscription Cancellations: You can cancel future renewals at any time directly through billing. Your premium quota benefits will remain active until the end of your current cycle.
  • 48-Hour Refund Request Window: Digital purchases are eligible for a refund request ONLY within 48 hours from the exact payment verification timestamp.
  • System Zero-Utilization Rule: Because active premium resource access generates immediate computational API costs, refunds are only issued if zero premium features (e.g. AI checklist dictations, grace protections) have been utilized during the transaction period.
  • Gateway Processing Deductions: Approved refunds are subject to a nominal 3% payment gateway processing fee deduction from the original amount paid.
⚠️ IMPORTANT: Submitting a request locks the Premium features of that card pending review. If approved, limits instantly fall back to the Free baseline. Rejections keep your Premium tier active until standard expiry.
