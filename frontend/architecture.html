Data Storage

We prioritize transparency in how your data is handled:

  • Personal Info: Stored in a secure MongoDB cluster. Encrypted in transit.
  • Images: Only profile and group icons are stored on Cloudinary. We do not store unrelated media.
  • Offline Sync: Uses IndexedDB to store your tasks locally. Data is cleared immediately upon logout.
  • Persistence: Session tokens (JWT) are stored in your browser's local storage securely.

Privacy Controls

You have full control over your visibility:

  • Public Profile: Can be toggled off at any time. When off, you are invisible to search and leaderboards.
  • Secure Sharing: Unique share codes expire in 30 days (10 mins for previews) to prevent permanent public exposure.
  • Group Privacy: Joining a group shares tasks with members for accountability. This is explicitly warned before joining.
  • Right to be Forgotten: "Delete Account" performs a hard-delete across all database tables and Cloudinary.

Optimization

Performance-driven architectural choices:

  • Bandwidth: Pagination on all lists (Tasks, Chat, Leaderboard) prevents heavy data downloads.
  • Latency: Stale-While-Revalidate caching strategy provides instant UI updates while syncing in the background.
  • Assets: All images are lazy-loaded and served through global CDNs for speed.
  • PWA: Service workers ensure the app remains functional even in zero-network conditions.

Real-time Features

Powered by Firebase for sub-second latency:

  • Live Chat: Firestore handles group messaging with minimal overhead.
  • Status Sync: Online/Offline presence and read receipts are updated in real-time.
  • Rate Limiting: All real-time operations are throttled to prevent spam and system abuse.
