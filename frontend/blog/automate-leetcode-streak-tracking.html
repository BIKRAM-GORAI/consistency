How to Automate Your LeetCode Streak Tracking (Without Manual Checklists)

✍️ Bikram Gorai ⏱️ 5 min read 📅 November 2026

Every software engineer and computer science student knows the pain: you finish a grueling 45-minute LeetCode problem, celebrate the green "Accepted" screen, and then forget to manually log it into your habit tracker or spreadsheet. A few days later, your streak tracker claims you quit, even though you coded every single day.

Manual tracking introduces friction. In behavioral psychology, friction is the number one killer of habit formation. If logging a habit takes effort after the work is done, you will eventually abandon the habit tracker.

The Problem with Manual Coding Checklists

Most developers use generic to-do apps like Todoist or Notion. While these are fine for buying groceries, they fail for software engineering discipline:

  • Honor-System Temptation: Checking a box without actually solving a problem is too easy on tired days.
  • Duplicate Effort: LeetCode already records your accepted submission timestamps. Why should you re-type it into another app?
  • Fragmented Stats: Your coding practice stays on LeetCode, your gym habits stay in another app, and your daily tasks live in a third place.

The Zero-Friction Solution: Direct GraphQL Sync

What if your habit tracker talked directly to LeetCode? That is the exact problem Consistency Daily solves. By leveraging LeetCode's public GraphQL API, the platform checks your public profile daily. When an accepted problem (AC) is detected, it automatically checks off your daily engineering card and increments your consistency streak.

How to set it up in 30 seconds:

1. Open your Consistency Daily profile settings.
2. Enter your public LeetCode username.
3. Toggle LeetCode Auto-Sync to ON. That's it! Your accepted problems now count toward your daily consistency streak automatically.

How This Transforms Interview Preparation

When you're preparing for technical interviews at FAANG or top startups, maintaining a 90-day daily problem-solving habit is more valuable than cramming 100 problems the weekend before. Automated syncing gives you:

  • Unbroken Momentum: See your LeetCode consistency displayed side-by-side with your fitness and reading goals on an interactive 52-week heatmap.
  • Streak Protection: Paired with Consistency Daily's Streak Grace feature, an unexpected interview travel day won't wipe out your hard-earned 60-day streak.
  • Public Proof: Share your verified streak on your public profile link to show recruiters your relentless daily work ethic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Consistency Daily require my LeetCode password?

No, never. It only requires your public username to inspect public submission logs via read-only GraphQL endpoints. Your account credentials remain 100% private.

What if LeetCode servers are slow or down?

Consistency Daily utilizes a dual-tier fallback mechanism (Direct GraphQL + mirror API) with retries, ensuring your streak is captured accurately throughout the 24-hour cycle.
