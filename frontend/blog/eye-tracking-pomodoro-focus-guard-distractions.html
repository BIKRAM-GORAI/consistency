The Active Eye-Tracking Pomodoro: Stopping Phone Distractions with Focus Guard

✍️ Bikram Gorai ⏱️ 6 min read 📅 November 2026

The Pomodoro Technique—working for 25 minutes followed by a 5-minute break—is legendary. But in 2026, it faces a fatal flaw: the smartphone in your pocket.

How many times have you started a 25-minute timer on your computer, only to unconsciously pick up your phone 4 minutes later to check a notification, look up, and realize the timer finished while you were doomscrolling Instagram or TikTok?

Why Traditional Timers Can Be "Cheated"

Conventional timers are passive clocks. They tick down regardless of whether you are writing high-performance code or daydreaming out the window. This produces false metrics: your app reports "4 hours of focus," but your actual deep work was barely 45 minutes.

How Focus Guard Eye-Tracking Works

Consistency Daily reimagines the focus timer with an optional, groundbreaking feature: Focus Guard.

By leveraging lightweight, browser-based computer vision (running entirely on client-side WebAssembly), Focus Guard tracks your gaze and head position:

  • Attentive Focus Detection: As long as your eyes are focused on your work screen, the timer progresses smoothly.
  • Instant Distraction Alert: If your gaze drifts down toward your lap (where you are holding your phone) or away from the monitor for more than a few seconds, the timer pauses and triggers a gentle alert.
  • True "Deep Work" Metrics: At the end of the day, your focus analytics reflect authentic, verified attention minutes rather than passive clock ticks.

100% Privacy-First: Zero Cloud Transmission

Your privacy is non-negotiable. All computer vision calculations occur strictly in your local device RAM/GPU. No video frames, images, or audio clips are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.

Who Benefits Most from Focus Guard?

  • ADHD Learners: Provides an immediate, external stimulus that redirects wandering attention before you get sucked into a dopamine loop.
  • Competitive Exam Aspirants: Prepares candidates for intense 3-hour exam environments where looking away is penalized.
  • Software Developers: Protects deep architectural flow state during complex debugging sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the Pomodoro timer without the camera?

Absolutely. Focus Guard is 100% optional. You can use the standard neubrutalist Pomodoro timer with custom audio chimes and interval settings anytime.

Does it drain heavy battery?

No, the on-device model is optimized for minimal CPU usage, sampling gaze cues efficiently without heating up your laptop.
