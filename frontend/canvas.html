Canvas Guide

🤖 AI Agent

✦
Type a prompt & send Describe your plan in natural language (e.g. "Build a morning routine with a condition for gym days"). The AI will generate your entire flow.
⇧↵
Shift + Enter Keyboard shortcut to send your message. Regular Enter inserts a new line.

✋ Manual Editing

▶
Action A step or task in your flow (e.g. "Wake up", "Study for 2 hours"). Shown in teal.
⎇
Condition (Cond.) A decision point with branching paths (e.g. "Did I pass?"). Shown in pink.
◎
Goal An end state or milestone you want to reach (e.g. "Get fit"). Shown in lime.
🔗
Connect Click "Connect" to enter linking mode. Then click the "+" port on any card border to start, and another "+" port to finish the connection arrow.

🖱️ Canvas Controls

✥
Pan the Canvas Click and drag on any empty area to move around the canvas.
⊕
Zoom In / Out Scroll your mouse wheel on the canvas, or use the +/- buttons in the bottom-left corner.
☰
Drag Nodes Grab the colored header bar of any card and drag it to reposition.
✎
Edit Labels Click the text inside any card to edit it directly. Tab or click away to save.

⌨️ Shortcuts & Modes

↩
Undo / Redo Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y to redo your last action.
⊞
Design Mode Build and edit your flow: drag nodes, add connections, edit text, and manage subtasks.
▷
Execute Mode Run your plan: nodes are locked in place. Tick subtasks to track progress, auto-completing nodes when all items are done.
⊟
Delete Connection In Design mode, click any arrow/line to get a delete prompt.
📐
Auto-Align Type "Align all nodes" in the chat to auto-arrange your flow left-to-right without duplicating anything.
