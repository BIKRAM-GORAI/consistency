Common Questions ❓

Is Consistency Daily really free?

+

Yes! The core platform is free for everyone to use. Our goal is to help students and professionals build better habits without any paywalls or hidden fees.

How private is my data?

+

Extremely. Your account, tasks, and streaks are strictly your own. Each user has an isolated private account, and your progress is only visible to you unless you choose to join a group.

Can I use it on my phone?

+

Absolutely. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA). Just click the 'Install App' button or 'Add to Home Screen' in your mobile browser for a native-app experience with offline support.

Does the LeetCode integration actually work?

+

Yes! You can paste the URL of the problem you solved in the dedicated LeetCode section. Click 'Validate', and our system will verify if you completed it that day before adding it to your daily tasks.

What happens if I miss a day?

+

Your streak will reset, but don't worry! Every new day is a fresh start. You can use our 'Save Template' feature to quickly set up your daily goals and get back on track instantly.
